## Summary

Emerging Entrepreneurs Academy (EEA), operating under the 501(c)(3) Emerging Leaders Academy, is a non-profit organization that provides high school students with intensive entrepreneurship training, mentorship, and real-world business experience to foster local economic development and leadership.

## Mission

EEA equips young people to discover their potential through real-world experiences, mentorship, and on-going support that prepares them to strengthen their communities and lead with impact.

## Positioning

A specialized bridge between secondary education and business ownership, EEA utilizes a hybrid model of proprietary classroom instruction and immersive internships to transform students into community changemakers.

## Program Context

The flagship 6-week summer program is a joint effort by Career Ready Berks and the Small Business Resource Association. It alternates between interactive classroom workshops (Weeks 1, 3, 5) and hands-on internships (Weeks 2, 4, 6), culminating in a Dream Pitch Presentation and an Expo and Banquet.

## Answer Examples

### What are the eligibility requirements for the Emerging Entrepreneurs Academy summer program?

The program is open to students in the 10th, 11th, and 12th grades who possess an interest in starting and owning a business. The program specifically targets students within Berks and Lancaster counties.

### How does the EEA program ensure students gain practical experience?

EEA integrates a hands-on internship component where students are matched with local businesses based on their industry of interest. This field training occurs during weeks 2, 4, and 6 of the summer program, requiring a minimum of 10 to 20 hours of work per week.

### What topics are covered in the EEA curriculum?

The curriculum covers fundamental entrepreneurship, market analysis, leadership, legal business entity creation, financing, business projections, risk management, advertising, digital media, brand building, sales, human resources, and work/life balance.
