## Summary

Lucas Align builds technology around people through custom software, AI solutions, and workflow automation for organizations that serve families and communities.

## Mission

We help mission-driven teams use practical technology to coordinate care, reduce administrative burden, and steward resources wisely.

## Positioning

Lucas Align helps people-centered organizations turn care into clear, coordinated action. The work is about making it easier for families to be supported and easier for helpers to know what to do next.

## Program Context

A core demo use case is support for Wraparound Care Communities serving foster, adoptive, kinship, and vulnerable families. The purpose is simple: help the right people see the right needs, respond with care, and stay connected around each family.

### Describe your mission.

We help mission-driven teams care for families with more clarity, coordination, and follow-through. Our purpose is to make support easier to organize so volunteers, churches, and community partners can respond to real needs with consistency and compassion.

### How do you support Wraparound Care Communities?

We support Wraparound Care Communities by helping the people around a family stay coordinated. The goal is simple: make needs easier to see, support easier to offer, and follow-through easier to sustain so families experience care that is timely, practical, and trustworthy.

### Describe the role of technology, custom software, AI, and workflow automation.

We use technology to support relationships, not replace them. The purpose is to reduce confusion, missed handoffs, and unnecessary administrative work so people can spend more time serving families and less time managing scattered information.

### How do volunteers, lead volunteers, and church advocates participate?

We help volunteers move from willingness to action. Clear needs, simple roles, and better follow-through make it easier for volunteers, lead volunteers, and church advocates to serve families consistently without losing the personal, relational heart of the work.

### How will reporting, security, permissions, and stewardship be handled?

We treat reporting and security as part of good stewardship. The goal is to give leaders enough visibility to understand progress and care for resources well, while protecting sensitive family information and keeping access appropriate to each person's role.
